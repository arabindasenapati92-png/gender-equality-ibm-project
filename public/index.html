KNOWLEDGE IS YOURS

You have rights.
Start with what you know.

Rules and services differ by country. These general starting points are for learning, not legal advice.

Explore UN Women resources ↗
01What can I do if I feel unsafe right now?+

Move toward a public place or someone you trust if you can. Contact your local emergency service or a local support organization. Use a device and communication method you believe are safe for you.

02How can I document an incident?+

If it feels safe, note what happened, when and where, and keep relevant messages or records. Consider sharing them only with someone you trust or a qualified local advocate. You decide what to share.

03Where can I find local legal or crisis support?+

Search for a local women's organization, legal aid provider, or domestic violence service. HotPeachPages maintains an international directory; check whether a service is available and safe to contact in your area.

04Can self-defense resources guarantee my safety?+

No. Personal safety is not your responsibility alone, and no technique can guarantee an outcome. A reputable local instructor or support organization can discuss options without blame or pressure.
